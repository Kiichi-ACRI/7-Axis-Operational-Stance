# Axis Definitions (v2.0)

## 1. Individual ↔ Collective
Degree to which agency is located in individuals vs groups.

## 2. Rule of Law ↔ Force
How legitimacy is enforced: through formal rules or coercive power.

## 3. Centralization ↔ Decentralization
Spatial distribution of authority.

## 4. Minimal ↔ Maximal Enforcement
Strength and scope of institutional intervention.

## 5. Irreversibility ↔ Technological Reset
Treatment of historical constraints and path dependence.

## 6. Real Axis ↔ Narrative Axis
Weight placed on physical institutions vs symbolic narratives.

## 7. Elite ↔ Popular Legitimacy
Source of political legitimacy.
